## The Obsessive Shadow is Coming to Xbox!
Hey guys! I just wanted to let you all know that The Obsessive Shadow is heading to Xbox! I very much look forward to releasing this for Xbox Series X|S AND Xbox One/One s consoles! Stay tuned on updates, but I look forward to putting it out on another platform for more of you guys to enjoy! 

Now that I have that out of the way, here's some other exciting news to cover!

---
## Mixed Reality: Home Invasion Mode!

This time the terror has followed you home… Based on the horrifying survival horror experience, The Obsessive Shadow is now invading the real world through Mixed Reality! What was once confined to the virtual world now bleeds into your own Home. With nothing but your eyes and a flashlight, you must survive the night as an unidentified stalker lurks within the Windows, of your actual home.

## Localization
I've had the honor of working with some very incredible folks who have joined the localization team! So far we have support for English (US), Spanish, and French subtitles & Interfaces! Subtitles/localization will be fully supported in both VR mode AND Non-VR/Flat mode so that it is fully accessible! It's really exciting to me that we were able to get the game translated so that more people can enjoy it to the fullest!

### Mobile Ports
---
Yes! Mobile ports are also happening! They will release in the near future on both iOS and Android, however right now there is some stuff to figure out with apple that is being worked through for the iOS version specifically. It is designed to run well on Majority of phones and tablets for Apple and Android alike and admittedly it's coming along very well so far. It also looks and feels really great to play on the brand new iPhone Duo!

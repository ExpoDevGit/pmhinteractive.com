The Obsessive Shadow

The Obsessive Shadow is a short survival horror game set in 1986 American Suburbs. You play as 9 year old Liam Robertson. While watching tv, the power mysteriously goes out. You quickly find out that your mother is missing when a strange phone call suddenly becomes your only hope at surviving the night. While only armed with a flashlight and a familiar voice on the phone, you must rely on your eyes, ears, and pure instinct to keep whatever is hunting you away.

Every hour that creeps by, the rules for survival change. You must adapt and overcome whatever the night throws your way. IT. IS. HERE. With 3 different endings and multiple achievements to unlock, you must push through and face the darkness. Will the increasingly odd instructions keep you alive? Or will this monster catch you when you least expect it?

Also available for VR arcades & commercial venues via SpringboardVR and SynthesisVR.

The Obsessive Shadow — Chapter 2

Two decades after the chilling horrors of the past, the year is 2006, and the abandoned streets of Old Diamond Drive lie shrouded in eerie mystery. Once a vibrant neighborhood, it’s now the heart of chilling urban legends whispered across town. 16 year old Justin, having lost a bet to his friend Matthew, accepts a daring challenge: spend a single night among the remains of this shell of a once thriving community. Armed with only a camcorder and a flashlight, he steps into the darkness, unaware of the nightmare awaiting him. As the shadows close in, Justin discovers he’s trapped on Old Diamond Drive... and he’s not alone. To survive, he must confront the horrors face to face and challenge the unknown figure lurking in the obscurity of the dark.

The Obsessive Shadow: Former Residents

Knock Knock? Who’s There? The Obsessive Shadow is lurking... In this 2D Horror Narrative, Uncover the story of what happened to the former residents of Old Diamond Drive. A once thriving community later finds itself left in ruin. Abandoned, neglected, and mostly forgotten... But how did this happen? Live the stories of the people living there and see for yourself. Each night, you’ll take on the role of someone being stalked, hunted, and watched. Watch your windows... Listen for knocking... and KEEP A FLASHLIGHT HANDY... The danger that lurks in the shadows can do more than you would think... IT. IS. HERE.

Experience a collection of interconnected stories spanning the week that changed everything, where every resident has their own encounter with the unseen force haunting the neighborhood. Look through darkened homes, uncover hidden clues, and piece together the truth behind the disappearances, tragedies, and strange events that transformed Old Diamond Drive into a ghost of its former self. What begins as an isolated incident soon reveals a far more disturbing reality one that has been watching from the darkness all along.

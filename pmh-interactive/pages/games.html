The Obsessive Shadow

You take on the role of Liam, a 9-year-old boy left alone in his home after a sudden power outage plunges the house into suffocating darkness. Armed with only a flashlight, you must navigate the unsettling dangers of the night as you are being watched. Your mother's voice on the phone is your guide to surviving — but as her instructions grow more confusing and the unease deepens, the safety of her words begins to feel increasingly fragile.

Also available for VR arcades & commercial venues via SpringboardVR and SynthesisVR.
